Questions, answered

Is this actually free?

Yes, completely. No paywall, no account, no trial period. If a worksheet helps and you'd like to support the work, there's an optional donation link, but it's never required.

Do I need to sign up or create an account?

No. Just download the PDF you need. Subscribing to the newsletter is optional, only for updates and new worksheets, not required to use anything.

Is this therapy or a replacement for professional support?

No. These worksheets are a self-help and self-reflection tool for everyday workplace challenges, not a substitute for therapy, counselling, or crisis support. If you're going through something serious, please reach out to a licensed professional.

Is what I write actually private?

Completely. These are downloadable PDFs you fill in yourself, on your own device or on paper. Nothing is sent back to us, stored, or seen by anyone at WorkITout.

Will there be more worksheets?

Yes, more are on the way. Subscribe to the newsletter or follow along on LinkedIn to know as soon as new ones are out.
